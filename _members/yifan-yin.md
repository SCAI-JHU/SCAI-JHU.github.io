My research is at the intersection of embodied AI, 3D vision, robotics, and human-robot interaction. My recent work focuses on (1) 3D world modeling for anticipating and evaluating embodied state changes; (2) robot learning via multimodal reasoning and experience reflection; and (3) integrated task and motion planning for embodied assistance and human-robot collaboration.
