Welcome to the Social Cognitive AI (SCAI, pronounced “sky”) Lab at Johns Hopkins University, led by Dr. <a href="https://www.tshu.io/">Tianmin Shu</a>. We study machine social intelligence: building AI agents that can understand, reason about, and interact with humans and other agents in complex, open-ended environments. Our interdisciplinary approach combines methods from embodied AI, machine learning, robotics, and computational social cognition. We are particularly interested in agents that learn from embodied and social experiences, build models of the world and other minds, and cooperate with humans and machines over long horizons.
